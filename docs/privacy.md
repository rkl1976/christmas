# Privacy policy: Desktop Hygge (Microsoft Store)

*Last updated: 21 November 2026*

Desktop Hygge is the Microsoft Store version of JuleHygge, a seasonal overlay that draws snow, Christmas lights
and small figures on your desktop.

## What the app collects

Nothing. Desktop Hygge does not collect, store or send any personal information.

- **No network connections.** The Store version never connects to the internet. Updates come through the
  Microsoft Store.
- **No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.**
- **Your windows:** to let snow settle on top of your open windows, the app reads where your windows are on the
  screen and what kind of window they are. It only uses this in memory while it runs, to draw the snow. It never
  reads what is inside your windows, and it never saves or sends any of it.
- **Settings** (your choices in the menu) are saved in a small file in the app's own folder on your PC.
- **Error log:** if the app hits an error, it writes a short technical note (the error message, no personal
  data) to a file in your PC's temporary folder. It stays on your PC.

## The Microsoft Store

Microsoft handles downloads, updates and payments through the Store under its own privacy statement:
<https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement>. The developer does not receive information about you from these.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Privatlivspolitik (dansk)

Desktop Hygge indsamler, gemmer og sender ingen personoplysninger. Store-udgaven forbinder sig aldrig til
internettet; opdateringer kommer gennem Microsoft Store. Appen læser, hvor dine vinduer står på skærmen, så sneen
kan lægge sig på dem, men kun i hukommelsen, mens den kører, og aldrig hvad der står i dem. Dine indstillinger
gemmes i en lille fil på din pc, og en eventuel fejlbesked skrives i pc'ens midlertidige mappe. Intet af det
forlader din pc. Microsoft står for download, opdatering og betaling efter sin egen privatlivserklæring.
Spørgsmål: <https://github.com/rkl1976/christmas/issues>.
