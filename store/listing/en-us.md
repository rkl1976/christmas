# Store listing: English (en-us)

## Short description

Snow falls over your desktop and settles on your windows, Christmas lights glow, Santa flies by and children build a snowman, while you work.

## Description

Bring a little winter magic to your desktop. Desktop Hygge lets snow fall gently over your screen while you work. It settles on the taskbar and piles up on top of your open windows, Christmas lights glow along the top of the screen, and little Christmas elves stroll along the bottom, stopping now and then to swap gifts.

Every few minutes something happens. Santa flies across the sky in his sleigh, pulled by reindeer and trailing golden sparkles. A snowplow rumbles out to clear the snow when it gets deep. Three children in winter clothes build a snowman: they roll the balls, lift them onto each other and dress it with a carrot nose, buttons, a scarf and a knitted hat. When it is done they cheer and start a snowball fight, until the snowplow comes by and runs the snowman down. Then they pick a new spot and start all over again.

The weather changes through the day, from light flurries to a proper snowstorm that leaves drifts behind.

Desktop Hygge stays out of your way. The overlay is see-through and every click goes straight through to your apps. It pauses by itself while a game or film runs in full screen, and stops completely while your screen is locked or asleep.

Prefer autumn? Switch the theme and the snow turns into falling leaves, Santa into a flock of migrating geese, the snowplow into a leaf blower, and hedgehogs and squirrels take over the walk.

• Works on all your screens
